## Conventions

- **Accrual, not cash.** The Bank of Guyana reports inflows on an accrual basis.
  Press figures are generally cash. 2026Q1 inflows were US$577.60M accrual and
  US$761.70M cash; the difference is exactly the US$184.11M of December 2025
  lifts received 6 and 23 January 2026. This dataset uses accrual throughout.
- **Actual, not approved.** Withdrawals are summed from Table 7 transaction
  records, not from approved ceilings. The two coincide to 100.00% in 2022–2024
  and 99.96% in 2025, but they are separate columns in `nrf_annual_verified.csv`.
- **2026 is partial** — through 30 June 2026 only.
